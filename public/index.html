Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

iPromotion is a personal, non-commercial project run by Khoa Dinh in Oulu, Finland, who is the controller of the personal data described here (contact details on the Contact page). This page explains what the site collects, why, and what you can ask for.

Without an account

  • No tracking or ads. The site uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • In your browser only: starred categories and whether the sidebar is collapsed are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you sign in.
  • IP address: each request's IP address is used to limit abuse (for example, a few refreshes or photo checks per 10 minutes). Counters for costly actions are kept in our database for the length of that window and then deleted. Our hosting provider also keeps short-lived technical logs.
  • Deals assistant (signed in): your question is checked on our server and sent to an AI model on Hugging Face to search this week's deals and write the answer. Your conversation is saved on our server so you can come back to it, and deleted 24 hours after it started; type /clear in the chat to delete it sooner. Answers (without your name or account) are cached for up to 24 hours so the same question is answered faster. Questions are not written to our logs; we count how many you ask per day.
  • Location (Food section, optional): Wolt offers are shown for Oulu by default. Only if you press "Update location" and allow it, your browser's location is rounded to about 1 km and sent to our server to look up Wolt offers near you. The offers are cached for 6 hours per area; the area is not linked to you.

With a Google account

  • Account: signing in uses Google through Firebase Authentication. We store your name, email address, profile photo link, Google account ID, and when you signed up and last logged in, so we can keep your data and recognise you.
  • Favorites: the categories you star, so they follow you across devices.
  • Follow your discount: the dates you save (use-by or best-before), the product name and the location you type. Photos are shrunk in your browser and sent once to an AI model on Hugging Face to read the date; we do not store photos. We count your photo checks per day to keep the service free.
  • Checks and payments: how many photo checks you have, and a record of each change (free, used, bought). When you buy checks, we keep what you bought, the amount and the time, and Stripe's payment reference. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.

Why we use it (legal basis)

To provide the features you ask for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), with your consent for location and photo checks (Art. 6(1)(a)), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and free of abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)). We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Vercel: hosting; the server runs in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Neon: the database, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Google Firebase: sign-in.
  • Hugging Face and its inference providers: reading dates from photos.
  • Stripe: payments. Stripe is also a controller for payment data it must keep by law (see stripe.com/privacy).

Some of these companies are based in the United States; transfers rely on their standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

Account data, favorites and saved dates are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. Payment records are kept as long as accounting law requires (in Finland, generally 6 years), even if the account is deleted. Each account keeps at most 300 dates; the oldest is removed first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its use. Send your request by email (see Contact) and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Significant changes will be shown on the site.

What iPromotion is

A free, personal, non-commercial project that collects publicly available offers from store websites around Oulu and shows them in one place. iPromotion is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of the stores shown. Store names and logos are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify the stores.

Prices and offers

Offers are read automatically from the stores' own pages, as they appear when we collect them. They may be incomplete, outdated or wrong: a price, product, store branch or validity date can differ from what the store actually offers, and offers can end early or sell out. Always check the store's official website or brochure before visiting. The price and conditions in the store always apply. Campaign summaries in the Food section are written by an AI model and can contain mistakes.

Follow your discount

Dates are read from your photos by an AI model and can be wrong. Always check the date printed on the package. Do not rely on iPromotion for food safety decisions.

Photo checks and payments

Reading dates from photos uses checks. Every account gets free checks (3 to start and 1 more each week). A check is used only when a date is found. More checks can be bought on the pricing page: packs of checks that never expire, or Unlimited for 30 days (fair use applies). Payments are one-time, through Stripe; there is no subscription and nothing renews automatically. Prices are in euros and include VAT.

Checks are added to your account right after payment. By asking for that at checkout you agree that the 14-day right of withdrawal for digital content ends when they are added. If a payment or a check goes wrong because of a problem on our side, contact us and we will fix it or refund you. Checks can't be exchanged for money or moved to another account.
